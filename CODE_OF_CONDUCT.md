# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That document is the authority; the notes below are how it is applied here.

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is competent and acting
in good faith, and that anything you find obvious was not obvious to them.

Not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, or making someone
feel unwelcome because of who they are.

## What this looks like in a technical project

Disagreeing with a design is fine and useful. Kwery has changed direction several
times because someone argued it was wrong, and the reasoning is recorded in the
documentation rather than hidden. Criticise the decision, the code or the
measurement, not the person who made it.

"Why would you do it that way" reads as an attack. "What made you choose this
over X" asks the same question and gets a better answer.

If you report a bug, the most useful thing you can bring is a failing test. If
you cannot produce one, say so and describe what you saw. Nobody is owed a
reproduction, and nobody is owed a fix.

## Reporting

Email **dbjpanda@live.com**. Reports are handled privately.

Kwery is maintained by one person, so there is no committee and no appeals
process. What you get is that reports are read, taken seriously, and not
discussed with anyone who does not need to know.
